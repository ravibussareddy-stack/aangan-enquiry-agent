Aangan Studio · AI phone enquiries

How a call flows

The AI only picks up when the front desk is busy, doesn't answer, or the office is closed. It finds out why the person is calling, works through the intake sheet for new projects, and lets the server, never the model, decide the outcome. Price questions, and callers with no budget in mind, get the typical range for their scope (server-written, indicative, depends on locality and requirements) and a check that they're comfortable going ahead. The agent never says a figure of its own, and every call ends with "Have a good day!". It never promises when someone will call back: callers hear "as soon as possible" and are asked when they're generally free. Everything after the call happens automatically within seconds.

The three conversations below are text simulations: Gemini played the caller, from the studio's real September enquiries, against the live agent code. No Vaani credit was used.

5 · Tools used

What the agent can do on a call

The model chooses when to call these; the server decides the result and enforces the rules on the right.

ToolUsed whenWhat the server enforces
lookup_callerLook up the caller by phone number in the CRM. Returns whether they're an existing client, any open project, and anything captured on an earlier dropped call.Run by the server on the first reply (no model round). Finds existing clients in HubSpot.
escalate_complaintExisting client with a complaint: alert a senior person to call back as soon as possible (never promise a time). Call once, then end the call.Only for an existing Aangan client's own project; otherwise refused. Seniors alerted; no time promised.
check_scopeCheck the project against Aangan's hard rules (area, category, engagement, size, timeline). Returns in_scope, out_of_scope (with a close line) or uncertain (with what to ask).Hard rules (area, category, engagement, size, timeline) decide; returns the next intake question.
evaluate_leadFinal qualification once you've collected the details. Returns next_step: qualified, review, declined or redirect, with a say_line where needed.Blocks until scope is known and required intake fields are asked (≤2 nudges); confirms a volunteered budget was repeated back; routes to qualified / review / callback / declined.
typical_budgetThe caller asked about price, cost, rates, a ballpark or a quote. Returns the server-written typical range for their scope (with disclaimer) — say it exactly. Call it every time they ask; repeats come back shorter.Server writes the range + disclaimer; repeats are shorter. The only figures the agent may speak.
get_slotsGet 2–3 consultation times to offer, from designers' calendars over the next 7 days.Only after 'qualified'; waits for the caller's answer to the comfort check.
book_slotBook one of the slots returned by get_slots. Returns the confirmed time.Only a slot get_slots returned; books in Cal.com (or the mock calendar).
take_messageThe caller is NOT making a new enquiry: they want an update on an existing project, want to reach a specific designer, or something else (vendor, job, etc.). Take a short message and a callback time in their words; the right person gets it on Telegram. Then close the call.Free times must be quoted from the caller's words. Delivered to the named designer / helpdesk.
request_callbackRecord when the CALLER said they're generally free (review, callback and redirect leads). Ask first — never assume, and never promise a call-back time.Free times must be quoted from the caller's words.
transfer_to_front_deskCaller is unsure whether they want a project at all. In office hours returns a transfer; after hours creates a callback task for the next opening.In overflow mode: a callback task when the office opens — no promised time.
end_callEnd the call after your final sentence.Refused once on review / callback leads until free times are recorded. Server adds 'Have a good day!'.
